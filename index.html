02 — How it works

Four steps from idea to ink

  1. 01

    Tell us the job

    Call, WhatsApp, or walk in with your file, sample or a rough idea — no design ready is fine too.

  2. 02

    We design & proof

    Our team lays it out, picks paper and finish, and shows you a proof before anything goes to print.

  3. 03
